Battle Instructions

How to Play

Every action ends your turn. After you act, the boss gets a chance to attack.

01

Light Attack

A dependable attack that deals moderate damage and costs 20 stamina.

02

Heavy Attack

Deals greater damage and costs 40 stamina, but the attack can miss.

03

Dodge

Costs 30 stamina and gives you a chance to avoid the boss's next attack.

04

Healing Flask

Restores health. Your number of flasks is limited, so use them carefully.

05

Special Ability

Every class has a unique ability that can be used during the battle.

06

Boss Phase

The Ashen Warden becomes more dangerous after losing half of its health.
